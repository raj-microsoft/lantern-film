From an idea to 900 frames.

How this was made, following “Making the dots Film with Opus 5.5” by Ashok Reddy S, as credited in the AI Web Design Library. A new story and original assets, not a recreation of the dots film.

References

The guide supplies the production sequence and technical targets. Our visual references are everyday rituals: the school bag, a book by the door, an evening table. No third-party footage or character assets are used.

Script

One idea: a little less to carry. Six timed scenes move from morning to evening. The fictional assistant remembers a small thing, then waits for permission. Every line is in the script table above.

Rigs + font

Original parameterized 2D rigs: Lantern has a handle, breathing body and blinking eyes; the family has head, torso, arm and leg anchors. Pillow draws them. Self-hosted Figtree by Erik Kennedy, under the SIL Open Font License, gives film and page the same voice.

Footage + voice

Higgsfield and ElevenLabs are paid services, so neither was used. Python-generated morning and evening rooms replace footage. Voiceover is deliberately skipped; the planned voice lines are readable captions. All visuals are synthetic.

Scenes in code

One deterministic time function drives character movement, reminder arrival, permission state and transitions. Six compositions share a palette, window, tabletop and character rigs. The site’s shot controls cue the actual film.

Score in Python

NumPy synthesizes sine partials with attack and decay envelopes at 48 kHz. A 120 BPM arpeggio supports Cmaj7–Am7–Fmaj7–Gadd9. Stereo echoes add space; wave writes the PCM master.

Render

900 separately drawn RGB frames at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, streamed from Pillow to ffmpeg. No screen recording, stock clips or generated-video API. The poster and shot stills come directly from the same renderer.

Encode + check

H.264 / yuv420p with fast-start playback, stereo AAC at 192 kb/s. Two-pass ffmpeg loudnorm targets −16 LUFS, then the encoded MP4 is measured again. See the loudness report and media probe.

4K

The published master is native 1080p. A native 4K rendering path is included: python src/build_film.py --4k redraws every shape and letter at 3840 × 2160 rather than upscaling. The optional 4K export is not included in this release.
