From an idea to 900 frames.

How this was made, following “Making the dots Film with Opus 5.5” by Ashok Reddy S, in the full original guide, also listed in the AI Web Design Library. A new story and original assets, not a recreation of the dots film.

True claims, before the script

  • Lantern is an original fictional product concept, not a released AI service.
  • The film imagines a library-book reminder and shows a human approving it; it does not demonstrate a working assistant.
  • Every person, room and product character is synthetic and drawn in code, with no family data.
  • The score and cue effects are original synthesis; no voice or sampled recording is used.

00 · What you need

One Windows machine, a coding agent, Python with NumPy, Canvas 2D, headless Edge (Chromium), Playwright and ffmpeg. The workflow uses local tools without paid services, API keys or new accounts. Build entry point.

01 · Collect references

The full guide sets the sequence and technical targets. School bags, library books and an evening table shape our original visual world. The true-claims list above limits the story: this is a fictional proposal, never a claim of real AI capabilities.

02 · Pick the idea and write the script

“A little less to carry.” Six scenes move from morning to evening. A proposed reminder waits for a human click. The timed script table records every caption and intended voice line; all scene boundaries land on the half-second beat grid.

03 · Get the ingredients

Original Lantern and family rigs replace the guide’s official dots characters. The window, plant, tabletop and wordmark share one palette. Self-hosted Figtree by Erik Kennedy, SIL Open Font License, replaces the source brand font. Every character asset is original to this film.

04 · Footage

Higgsfield is a paid service, so original coded morning and evening rooms replace live-action footage. These synthetic 2D plates are drawn in the same scene functions as the rigs, with no downloaded video or real people.

05 · Voiceover

ElevenLabs is not used. Voice is deliberately omitted and every planned line becomes a secondary caption. The English text track and written script provide the full words without sound; the original instrumental mix supplies the mood.

06 · The film project

The whole film is one working web page. A deterministic seek(t) draws any instant directly, independent of playback history. The same time yields identical pixels. Inspect the scene code or use the preview’s time slider.

07 · Build the characters

Original Canvas rigs use squash and stretch, leaning bodies, independently anchored handles, blinks and small hops. Walking arms and legs share a beat phase. Flat cut-paper forms substitute for 3D plush fur and its 64 shells; the style remains intentionally simple and warm.

08 · Animate the six scenes

Every scene is a function of time. A 120 BPM pulse means one beat every 0.5 seconds: cards settle at 9.5 s, the cursor approves at 18 s, and the final character lands at 25.5 s. Springs resolve on those marks; gently moving plants and breathing poses keep held scenes alive.

09 · Music and sound

Python synthesizes five parts at 48 kHz: harmony, bass, arpeggio, melody and cue effects. Soft original keys replace the guide’s electro-pop to honor the calm brief. Original bell, wood and air effects are dedicated to CC0 and placed at every authored arrival, transition and approval cue. Cue sheet. Captions substitute for voice.

10 · Render and encode

Headless Edge, the installed Chromium browser, evaluates the web page 900 times at 30 fps. Four sub-frame samples are averaged over a 1/60-second exposure: real temporal motion blur, not CSS blur. Captions are drawn afterward for sharp edges. ffmpeg encodes H.264 / yuv420p and stereo AAC with fast-start playback.

11 · Check it

Independent ffprobe verifies 900 frames, 30 fps and 30 seconds. ffmpeg scans the encoded file for black or frozen intervals. Two-pass loudnorm targets −16 LUFS and measures the finished AAC mix again. Deterministic seek and changing motion-blur pixels are tested. Frame QC · Renderer checks · Loudness · Media probe.

12 · 4K master

The player serves the lighter 1080p master. The same browser project is also rendered natively at 3840 × 2160 with four sub-frame samples, not upscaled. Download the 4K master · 4K media probe.

13 · HyperFrames (optional)

HyperFrames is not used. The optional layered-timeline step is represented by readable scene functions, a timed caption track, and five separately downloadable audio stems. This is source-level separation, not a claimed HyperFrames project.
